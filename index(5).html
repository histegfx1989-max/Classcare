How the system works

A useful civic-data workflow is more than a form.

1. Collect

Capture role, state, school location, issue category, severity, description and evidence.

2. Verify

Let authorised reviewers mark reports as verified, duplicate, needs-more-evidence or closed.

3. Act

Route verified problems to the responsible authority and track status, response and resolution.

Recommended production architecture

For real public deployment, add a secure backend, role-based access, encrypted storage, malware/file validation, audit logs, rate limiting, consent/privacy notices, a review queue, evidence access controls, and a defined retention/deletion policy. Avoid publishing exact school coordinates or personal information unless there is a clear public-interest and legal basis.
